A major merit-based fellowship supporting full-time research Master's and Ph.D. students at the University of Manitoba. Students are normally considered through nomination by their home department or academic unit.

Postdoctoral researchers

Postdoctoral fellowships & research opportunities

Prospective postdoctoral researchers interested in Statistics, statistical machine learning, trustworthy AI, or related areas may be eligible for several competitive Canadian and Manitoba-based fellowship programs. Some programs require the applicant to identify a proposed supervisor or research project before applying.

Federal · Tri-agency

Canada Postdoctoral Research Award (CPRA)

Canada's major tri-agency postdoctoral research award, supporting outstanding early-career researchers across the natural sciences and engineering, health sciences, and social sciences and humanities. For Statistics and statistical machine-learning projects, NSERC will often be the relevant funding agency.

CPRA program ↗ Statistical sciences · Canada

CANSSI Distinguished Postdoctoral Fellowships

A two-year national fellowship program in the statistical sciences combining methodological research, collaboration, co-mentoring, teaching, and professional development. Fellows work with researchers at participating Canadian institutions.

CANSSI Distinguished Postdoctoral Fellowships ↗ Statistical sciences · Job board

CANSSI Postdoctoral Fellowship Opportunities

A regularly updated listing of postdoctoral positions in Statistics, biostatistics, data science, actuarial science, and related areas submitted by CANSSI member institutions across Canada.

Browse current postdoctoral positions ↗ Manitoba · Research fellowship

Research Manitoba Postdoctoral Fellowship

Competitive fellowship supporting postdoctoral researchers undertaking research in Manitoba. Eligible areas include Natural Sciences & Engineering, Health Sciences, and Social Sciences & Humanities.

Research Manitoba fellowship ↗ International researchers · Canada

Mitacs Accelerate Global Excellence Award

A competitive postdoctoral opportunity designed to attract outstanding international researchers, including Canadians currently studying abroad, to undertake collaborative research in Canada with an academic supervisor and partner organization. Priority technology areas include artificial intelligence, quantum technologies, cybersecurity, and other emerging fields.

Mitacs Global Excellence Award ↗ Academic–industry research

Mitacs Accelerate for Postdoctoral Researchers

Supports collaborative research projects involving postdoctoral fellows, university researchers, and eligible industry or not-for-profit partners. This can be particularly useful for applied AI, data-science, and interdisciplinary research projects.
